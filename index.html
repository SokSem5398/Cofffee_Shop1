About Us

A quiet corner built around the ritual of good coffee.

Nebo Café began as a single roaster's cart and grew into a neighborhood sanctuary. We source directly from small-lot farms, roast in-house, and pull every shot with intention. Every cup is a small act of craft.

  • Direct-trade, single-origin beans
  • Roasted fresh weekly, on site
  • Locally baked pastries, daily
